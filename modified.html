⚙ Settings

Order

Off: every scramble takes a random case and a random layer. On: the selected ones come one by one, each once, in random order. With both on, the run lasts until the longer list is through; the shorter one starts over.

CF cases you want to get

Click a letter to take the whole set. Click the number under it to pick individual cases.

Layers you want to get, and the solution you will do

Pictures as in the CF layers sheet: white on the bottom, green front-left (the last six: green on top, blue front-left). Angle numbers count from that holding. The scramble is built so that the ticked solution leads to one of your CF cases.
