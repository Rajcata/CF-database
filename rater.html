#Rater 2MovesAlgorithmIntended execution

ft / ut / bt / dt = thumb front / up / back / down, written at the start and after each regrip. z and z' are rotations inside the algorithm; after one the moves are written as they are turned from the new angle. A star marks a move done by the left hand in a special way: R'* and B'* with the right thumb lifted, b'* with the left ring finger.
